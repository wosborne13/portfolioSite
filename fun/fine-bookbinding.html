Fine Bookbinding

Over the past couple of years I've been teaching myself a more traditional form of bookbinding using leather covers. Once the leather is on the book, I use antique brass stamps, heated up and pressed into the covers and spine — sometimes with gold foil, sometimes just blind. It's been a fun learning curve.

Overview

Each book starts as a hand-sewn text block, laced onto boards and covered in leather. The tooling comes last: heating individual brass stamps and pressing them into the damp leather by hand, one impression at a time, building up borders and motifs across the cover and spine. Some get gold foil under the stamp, some are left blind for a subtler, pressed-in texture.
