About

Who I am

I'm Wil, a product designer and front-end developer based in the mountain west. I spend most of my time turning messy workflows — ledgers, booking calendars, content queues — into interfaces people don't have to think about. Outside of client work, I build small, slightly ridiculous side projects to keep my hands moving.

I've spent the last several years working with fintech, travel, and media teams, usually joining early enough to help shape the product direction, not just the pixels.

What I do

  • Design systems and component libraries
  • Front-end development (React, vanilla JS)
  • Prototyping and usability testing
  • Brand-adjacent visual design
